# Gallery kit: routines

Set at install. Times are the client's choice; these are the defaults.

| Bot | Routine | Default schedule | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inquiry Desk | Inbox watch | Every 30 minutes, 9 a.m. to 7 p.m., every day | Reads new inquiry emails and website form submissions, drafts replies, logs touches |
| Inquiry Desk | Daily digest | 6 p.m. daily | One message to the director: what came in, what's drafted, what needs a decision |
| Collector Follow-up | Opening follow-up | On request, the morning after an opening | Processes the uploaded sign-in book, drafts follow-ups |
| Collector Follow-up | Weekly quiet-list | Monday 8 a.m. | Lists contacts with no touch in 90 days and drafts a light check-in for the top ten |
| Consignment Tracker | Term watch | Daily 8 a.m. | Flags agreements ending within 30 days and drafts the renewal or return note |

Every routine ends with a draft or a Notion row. None of them send.
