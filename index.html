FAQ

Common questions,
straight answers.

What exactly is a fractional AI officer?
Think of it as a part-time Chief AI Officer embedded in your team. You get senior-level AI strategy, vendor evaluation, team training, and hands-on implementation — without the $300K+ salary, equity, and 6-month hiring process. You get the expertise on the days you need it, and nothing on the days you don't.
How quickly can we start seeing results?
Most clients see their first measurable win within 90 days. The first 2–3 weeks focus on auditing your current setup and building a prioritized roadmap. By week 4, we're typically shipping the first automation or custom agent. Quick wins come fast — transformational results compound over the first two quarters.
We already use ChatGPT and some AI tools. How is this different?
Using AI tools and having an AI strategy are two very different things. Most teams are using 10% of what's possible — and often the wrong 10%. We audit what you have, identify gaps and redundancies, and build a cohesive system where every tool and workflow connects. The goal is AI that works as an integrated layer across your business, not a collection of one-off experiments.
Will this replace anyone on our team?
No — and that's by design. Our approach is about augmenting your people, not replacing them. We train your team to be AI-fluent and build tools that make them faster, more creative, and more effective. The companies that win with AI are the ones that empower their people, not eliminate them.
What industries do you work with?
We work best with creative agencies, media companies, professional services firms, and mid-size businesses ($5M–$50M) that are operationally mature enough to benefit from AI but don't have the internal expertise to drive it. That said, the frameworks we use are industry-agnostic — if your workflows involve content, data, communication, or operations (they do), AI can improve them.
What's the minimum commitment?
We ask for a 3-month initial engagement to ensure we have enough time to deliver real results. After that, it's month-to-month with 30 days notice. We earn your business every month — if we're not delivering value, you should leave. (Our retention rate suggests that's not a problem.)
How is this different from hiring an AI consultant?
Traditional consultants hand you a strategy deck and wish you luck. We embed into your team, build the actual systems, train your people, and stick around to iterate. We don't just tell you what to do — we do it with you, and we're accountable for the outcomes. No 80-page reports that collect dust. Just working systems that move your business forward.
